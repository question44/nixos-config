# SYS-096 — Keep apps launched from Noctalia out of noctalia.service's cgroup

Outcome: Helium had been killed (SIGTRAP, "GPU process isn't usable") when an
activation restarted Noctalia, because apps launched from Noctalia lived in
its cgroup. `[shell] launch_apps_as_systemd_services = true` in
`home/noctalia/config.toml` (live 2026-11-09 19:08) now starts each app as its
own `app-<id>@<uuid>.service`. Verified 2026-11-10: Helium and Dolphin
launched from Noctalia run in their own units, Dolphin survived a Noctalia
crash and Helium a `systemctl --user restart noctalia.service`. Not tested: a
restart caused by an activation (expected to behave the same). Evidence:
[the changelog entry](../SYSTEM_CHANGELOG.md#noctalia-launches-apps-as-systemd-services--2026-11-09).
